# Story

Five beats, mapped to the page. Write them into `brief.json` under `story` before any code. Each beat has one feeling. Two adjacent beats with the same feeling is a fail; change one.

| Beat | Where | Person | Product | Company | Place |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| hook | hero, frames 0 to 100 percent | a face or hands, one line of who | the object turning, one line of what | the mark or the abstract, one line of promise | the walkthrough, one line of where |
| context | first band, the lede | why you do this | why it exists | who it is for | what makes it different |
| journey | the list section | 3 to 5 works | 3 to 5 features | 3 to 5 things you do | 3 to 5 rooms or spaces |
| climax | the peak band | one belief, big type | one detail nobody else has | one proof, a real one | the best room or view |
| resolution | the close | say hello | get one | talk to us | book a visit |

## Design Read
One line, written by the skill: "It's the site where ___." Must describe an experience, not a device. "It's the site where the shoe turns while the specs pin to it" is right. "It's the site with a scroll video" is wrong.

## Cues
Hero text uses `data-cue="start,hold,end"` as fractions of hero progress. The look sets the numbers. Hook enters first, sub second, both gone before 0.8 so the last frames play clean. Never more than 3 cued elements.

## Peak
The climax gets the signature move and the most generous spacing. It also gets silence before it: the section above ends with a plain line, no ornament.

## Copy
Short. Verbs. No banned words. If a fact is unknown, leave it as a `{{TODO}}` and list it for the user. Never invent clients, numbers, awards, or quotes.
